Fountain Coach · executable artistic capabilities

Instruments

Instruments make, hear and present artistic work. They have independent implementations, observable behaviour, and a place in a composition.

The instrument is not its library, its transport or its historical framework. What matters is what it can do, how it can participate, and what has actually been demonstrated.

The active instrument field

Three distinct acts.

These are developing software capabilities at different acceptance stages—not three already certified MIDI 2.0 instruments.

  • Sing01 · synthesisA voice becomes an instrument: phonemes, notes, pressure and time produce sound.
  • Ear02 · listeningSound is heard and measured, rather than treated as a file that merely exists.

Present implementation

What is actually running?

Sing · a voice

An original, native singing synthesizer whose phonetic states are played through typed MIDI 2.0 events. The persistent audio renderer has produced PCM and a macOS output witness.

Source and current evidence ↗ · The Ear That Learns · essay ↗
Development instrument. Native synthesis and typed-note rehearsal verified. Perceived intelligibility, full real-time performance and physical MIDI reception remain to be accepted.

Ear · a listening boundary

An AudioWorklet observation of sound, with measured levels and connections to a browser-local MIDI 2.0 event plane. It can participate in an instrument's feedback, without masquerading as an arbitrary microphone.

Worklet witness and limits ↗ · Listening and teaching · essay ↗
Research and integration. Real browser observations demonstrated; a separately admitted physical instrument and iPad Safari acceptance are not yet established.

SatzfunkPlayer · a published-image decision

A reusable listening player with Semantic Poster Imaging: select and compose a legible, source-bound image for the player, catalogue and social sharing. The poster is editorial work, not a random decoded frame.

Hear the published Satzfunk archive ↗
Software instrument candidate. Independent source and eight tests exist. Automated frame extraction, semantic vision and poster publication are still to be implemented; not yet an admitted MIDI 2.0 instrument.

Operating context

What makes an instrument?

An instrument has a declared purpose, bounded inputs and outputs, a responsible source owner, and evidence of its behavior. A MIDI 2.0 instrument must additionally meet its governed profile, lifecycle and transport obligations. Instruments is a supporting capability catalogue; MIDI2 documents the shared operational plane, Teatro composes, and Satzfunk publishes admitted hearings.
